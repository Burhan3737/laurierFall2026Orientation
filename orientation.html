Your action list

Ordered by urgency. Everything here comes from the same Laurier pages cited on the cards above.

  1. 01
    Email aspire@wlu.ca about Graduate Student Orientation (Sept. 2)

    Registration closed Aug. 20 at noon. The schedule page states: “If you did not register but would still like to attend, please email aspire@wlu.ca to see about availability.” Do this first.

  2. 02
    Email MACAdvising@wlu.ca about a Master of Applied Computing welcome

    MAC is absent from the published Program and Faculty Welcomes list. That address reaches Dr. Bernard Chiu (Graduate Officer) and Dr. Usama Mir (Program Coordinator).

  3. 03
    RSVP for the International Student Welcome Breakfast (Sept. 9)

    Free, international and exchange students only, Eventbrite RSVP. This is where you meet your International Student Leader in person.

  4. 04
    Book your SIN Clinic appointment (Sept. 14)

    Run on campus with Service Canada. As a co-op student you cannot be paid on a work term without a Social Insurance Number, so treat this as required rather than optional.

  5. 05
    Get tickets for the International Welcome Back BBQ (Sept. 17) and the Niagara Falls trip (Sept. 19)

    Both are Eventbrite-ticketed and capacity-limited. Halal, vegan and gluten-free options are confirmed for the BBQ.

  6. 06
    Complete the mandatory Fall 2026 Arrival Survey

    International Student Support requires it to be submitted seven days before you arrive in Canada. Not an event, but not optional either.

  7. 07
    Upload your OneCard photo before you land

    Your graduate OneCard is what gets you free entry to the Sept. 12 football game (East Gate only) and it is required for the grocery store tour.

  8. 08
    Catch up on the ASPIRE “Laurier Crash Course” recordings

    All ten Fall 2026 sessions have already run, but recordings sit inside the accordions on the Incoming Graduate Student Support page. “Ready to Thrive: Starting Strong at Laurier” (Aug. 19) is the most useful one for you now.
